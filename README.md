## A simple practice loop

1. Choose one short sentence and one focus, such as a final consonant or a stressed word.
2. Listen to a reference where available, then say the sentence at a comfortable pace.
3. Record and replay yourself before requesting a score.
4. Check whether a problem comes from pronunciation, a missed word, or poor recording conditions.
5. Make one change and compare a second recording. Write down the next focus.

The objective is understandable speech in context, not a perfect number on every recording. A higher automated score is not, by itself, proof of better communication.

## About Pronunciation Coach

[Pronunciation Coach](https://aipronunciationcoach.com/) is a browser-based tool for practising **English pronunciation with short, supplied sentences**. Its public product pages describe recording and playback, sentence-level pronunciation feedback, available word/sound feedback, and guided practice plans.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I check my English pronunciation online?

Use a tool that clearly tells you what to read, lets you replay the recording, and explains what its feedback means. In Pronunciation Coach, sign in, read a supplied sentence, record, replay, and then submit for assessment. See the [five-minute routine](guides/five-minute-practice.md) for a practice method that also works with an ordinary recorder.

### Can I practise for free?

The exercises and log in this repository can be used with your own recorder without an account. The hosted application's free assessments require Google sign-in and have daily and shared service limits. “Free” does not mean unlimited.

### Can I paste my own text into Pronunciation Coach?

No. The hosted checker uses supplied practice sentences. The extra exercises in this repository are for independent rehearsal; they are not promised inputs to the website's assessment feature.

### What does a low score mean?

It may reflect a pronunciation issue, missed text, or a recording/recognition problem. Replay the audio and inspect specific feedback before drawing a conclusion. See [understanding feedback](guides/understanding-feedback.md).

### Does the audio stay on my device?

Not after you submit it for assessment. The [privacy policy](https://aipronunciationcoach.com/privacy) explains external speech processing and the assessment/usage information retained by the service. Avoid recording sensitive information or other people without permission.
