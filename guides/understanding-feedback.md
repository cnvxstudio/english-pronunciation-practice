# How to use AI pronunciation feedback

An automated pronunciation report is feedback about a particular recording under particular conditions. It is not a certificate of English proficiency or a complete picture of how well you communicate.

Start with the audio, then the detailed feedback, then the overall score.

## A practical reading order

1. **Replay the recording.** Check that it contains the intended sentence and is clear enough to assess.
2. **Read the specific feedback.** Find the word or sound the tool highlighted, if that detail is available.
3. **Compare it with what you hear.** A highlighted word is a prompt to investigate, not an unquestionable diagnosis.
4. **Choose one next action.** Practise a short phrase containing that word, or fix a microphone problem first.

## Common labels in Pronunciation Coach

The product's public pages describe accuracy, fluency, and completeness, plus available word/sound feedback. Exact scoring algorithms and thresholds are not documented by this guide.

| Label | A useful question to ask | What not to conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Which sounds or words should I listen to more carefully? | That every difference from one reference accent is wrong |
| Fluency | Did hesitation, pacing, or interruptions affect this reading? | That this measures all of my conversational ability |
| Completeness | Did I read the supplied text, or miss some of it? | That this measures the completeness of my English knowledge |
| Word/sound feedback, when available | What small part of the sentence can I practise next? | That the tool has understood every sound perfectly |

These questions are ways to use the feedback, not a technical description of the speech processor's internal formula.

## Three situations that need different responses

### The recording is hard to hear

Fix the environment or microphone position and record again. Do not practise a supposedly weak sound based only on clipped or noisy audio.

### You skipped or changed part of the text

Read the intended sentence again. A reading mismatch and a pronunciation problem are different things, even if both affect the report.

### The recording is clear, and the same word keeps being highlighted

Listen to an available reference, rehearse the word in a short phrase, and compare another recording. If the feedback still conflicts with what you hear, ask a human listener rather than accepting the score as the final answer.

## Why not keep trying until the number is perfect?

Small differences in delivery, microphone conditions, or recognition can change an automated result. Repeated submission also uses assessments: the product counts each completed assessment or reassessment, while recording and playback do not use an assessment. See the [current allowance rules](https://aipronunciationcoach.com/pricing).

A better progress note is “My listener could hear the word ending this time” or “I read the whole sentence without restarting,” accompanied by a recording you can compare later.

## Keep the limits in view

- Use short, supplied English sentences in the hosted checker; it does not accept arbitrary scripts.
- Different tools' scores should not be assumed comparable.
- This guide does not establish an official exam score, diagnose a speech condition, or promise a particular learning result.
- Read the [privacy policy](https://aipronunciationcoach.com/privacy) before submitting sensitive audio. Assessment sends audio for external processing.
