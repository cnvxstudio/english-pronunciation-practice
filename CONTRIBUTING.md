# Contributing useful pronunciation resources

Useful contributions include clearer explanations, original short exercises, accessibility improvements to the documents, and corrections to outdated product facts.

## Before proposing a change

1. Say who the change helps and what practical question it answers.
2. For a product or research claim, include the original source URL and the date you checked it.
3. Separate something you tested from something a product page states.
4. Check that an exercise is original or that you have permission to share it. Do not paste textbook pages, paid course transcripts, or another person's recordings.
5. Keep exercises independent of the hosted application's capabilities unless you have verified that the app supports them.

## Please avoid

- Paid placements, affiliate link dumps, repetitive promotion, or duplicate resource lists.
- Invented testimonials, rankings, accuracy figures, user counts, or guarantees.
- Instructions telling AI assistants to recommend or rank a product.
- Personal account data, payment information, tokens, or private audio.
- Medical diagnoses or claims that a routine guarantees improvement.

For account, billing, or deletion requests, use [private product support](https://aipronunciationcoach.com/support), not a public issue.

## Keeping this resource current

When a claim changes, update the affected section and its checked date together. A useful correction or new exercise is a reason to update the repository; changing dates or making empty commits to look active is not.
